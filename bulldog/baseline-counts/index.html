One Day on Boulder's Streets

Cars, bus riders and bikes per day, from the city's own counters. The city publishes the bike number as a percentage change. Here it sits beside the cars.

Three counters, one day

Cars and bikes are counted at one point each on Baseline Road. The bus figure is every rider boarding anywhere on the whole HOP line, which is generous to the bus.

The bike counter, month by month

Average bikes per day at Baseline and Inca, both directions, March 2016 to August 2026, from the counter’s raw rows. The shaded windows are the two periods the city compared to get its 37.8 percent. The dashed lines are the averages inside them.

City's before and after windowsBikes per day, monthly average

The counter's before window averaged 134 a day and its after window 190, a rise of about 42 percent on the raw rows. The city's page says 37.8 percent and does not print either base. The counter was recording in 2019 too, when the road had ordinary painted lanes, at 181 a day.

What this is not. A survey. The city's 2023 travel diary says residents make about 18 percent of their trips by bike and 4 percent by bus. Those are self-reported trips on every path and street in town. This page is what passed three counters.
