    Method

    What this map does not claim

    • It records positions, filings and published rosters. It attributes no motive to any person.
    • Co-endorsement is not coordination. Where an endorsement coalition is described, it is in the words of the people who built it. No coordinated spending is alleged anywhere.
    • Boulder caps gifts to candidate committees at $100, so every candidate-cycle gift on the map is a capped gift. Concentrated money appears only where a ballot measure or an independent committee is uncapped.
    • One person who appears on both the Better Boulder and Boulder Progressives pages is not drawn, because the two pages never link the two names.
    • Rosters move. Board pages were last read in August and September 2026; committee filings run through the 2025 cycle.
    Not yet on the record

    What would change this map before November 3

    • 2026 endorsements from Better Boulder and Open Boulder. Neither had posted at the last check; both co-host the candidate forums with PLAN-Boulder County.
    • The 2026 committee filings in the city's election archive.
    • Colorado Secretary of State records for both Open Boulder entities and for any Boulder Progressives entity.
