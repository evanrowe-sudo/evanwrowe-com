Who paid, who won

Stock moves: Nov 4 close vs. Nov 3 close.

Oil & gas

Gave 97% of its $188 million in federal campaign money to Republicans. Republicans lost both chambers. Next day, U.S. crude oil fell 8.4%.

Hospitals

Gave to incumbents in both parties. Next day: HCA +10%, Centene +13%. Their revenue runs on Medicaid.

AI

Companies that sell computing power fell (Nvidia −3%, CoreWeave −10%). Companies that buy it rose (Apple +5%, Microsoft +7%, Amazon +6%).

AI: safety, or a price floor?

September 2026: OpenAI and Anthropic called for the AI industry to slow down. Together.

Anthropic’s CEO asked Washington for an antitrust waiver, permission for competitors to coordinate. If every lab slows down at once, nobody has to cut prices.

The White House said no:
“Stop pretending antitrust law has to be suspended so you can form a cartel.”
David Sacks, co-chair of the President’s Council of Advisors on Science and Technology

Questions

  1. Hedging. Defense contractors gave to both parties, and their stocks fell anyway. Is giving to both sides insurance or bribery?
  2. Safety or price floor? When AI companies ask to slow down together, is that safety, a cartel, or both?
  3. Who decides where AI gets built? The town, the state, or Congress? Who pays the power bill?
  4. The market as a voter. The Dow had its best day after a midterm since 1982. Whose vote is that?
