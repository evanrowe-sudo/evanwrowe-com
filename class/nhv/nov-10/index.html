Talk it through

  1. The general. Eisenhower ran the biggest military operation in history. Why would he be the one to warn about this?
  2. The pitch. “95,000 employed.” Is a weapon worth building because of the jobs it creates?
  3. The favor. Congress cut one Lockheed plane and gave the money to another Lockheed plane. Who actually lost?
  4. The door. If you expect to work for a contractor later, can you bargain hard with it now? Would you?
  5. The bill. Pick one person in today’s clips who pays. Did they choose anything?
  6. The new complex. AI is now sold as national security. Is a data center the new F-22?
  7. The citizenry. Eisenhower said only an alert and knowledgeable citizenry can keep this in check. Who is that today?
