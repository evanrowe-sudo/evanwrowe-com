Race and class, part two
Pieces 1 to 6 are Reed and Michaels. Pieces 7 to 14 are Catherine Liu. Pause any time.

    Questions

    1. Colleges track the race of who they admit. Why don’t they make the same promise about income?
    2. A laid-off white nurse: where is her share of “white wealth”? So what is the wealth gap measuring?
    3. Two workers earn the same wage, and both are below the poverty line. Is closing the gap between them justice?
    4. Liu says class is not an identity. If it is not an identity, what is it?
    5. Talarico and Hinojosa won in Texas without the trauma script. What did they talk about instead?
    6. Who gains when politics is argued in the language of suffering?
    Who gains when a problem is described by race instead of by class?
